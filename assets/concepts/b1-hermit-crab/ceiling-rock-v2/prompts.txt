생성된 네 발판을 개별 투명 PNG로 잘랐다. 중앙 암석은 이전 자산을 같은 비율로 약 25% 줄이고 합성본에서 아래 끝을 보스 중심 위에 놓았다. 안쪽 발판의 간격을 줄여 암석 타격 위치에 더 가깝게 배치했다.

발판 시트
Create exactly one transparent 2x2 sprite atlas of four redesigned original side-scroller cave platforms for RelicEscape. Reference 1 is our B1 room placement concept, to preserve the role and proportions of the four old horizontal platforms and match its teal cave colors. Reference 2 is our own low moss decoration, for the project's rounded leaf marks. Translate classic hand-inked dark garden cavern principles into original platforms: near-black ink silhouettes with organically scalloped undulating edges; dark muted blue-teal overlapping slab masses with only a few short highlight strokes; many individual small round olive-teal leaves grouped in irregular clusters along the top edge; selected thin hanging bead vines and asymmetrical dark root silhouettes below. Keep the walkable top flat and unbroken, with moss tufts on a few edge pockets only. Use small handmade imperfections and broken short highlight lines. Two wide platforms on the top row: left-wall support with its broad wall-facing end on the LEFT and thinner stepped tip on the RIGHT, right-wall support broad on the RIGHT and tapering to the LEFT, visually distinct from each other, long at about 4.8:1 width to rock height. Two short standalone stepping ledges in the bottom row about 3:1, left one shallow with one small dangling plant, right one shaped by uneven curved layers and a small pair of hanging leaves. Make the underside darker than the old art and planted ledges organic, avoid polished bright outlines, uniform green turf, symmetric bilateral shapes, bevel-heavy 3D, photoreal rendering or copied recognizable game assets. Front elevation. Generous empty clear gutters all around; do not let objects, vine tips, or roots touch cell edges or each other. Genuinely transparent RGBA everywhere outside the four assets; no white matte, cast shadows, gray background, borders, captions, rails, boss, stone target, room or floor.
