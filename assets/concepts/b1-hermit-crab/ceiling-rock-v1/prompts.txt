내장 ImageGen의 투명 배경 모드로 상단 발판 시트와 중앙 암석을 생성했다.
구도는 사용자가 첨부한 B1 스케치의 좌우 긴 발판, 안쪽 짧은 발판, 중앙 암석을 따른다.
RelicEscape의 nest-room-v2/room-preview.png를 원본 색·재질 참고로 사용했다.
HK-GP05의 비어 있는 보행면, HK-GP07의 읽기 쉬운 큰 돌 실루엣, SS-MG-02의 낮은 이끼 덩어리를 참고했다.
발판 시트에서 네 자산을 잘라 배경 잔여 알파를 정리했다. 합성 시안에서는 둥지 v2 레이어와 기존 유물 레일, 수정이 드러나기 전의 보스 등껍질을 재사용했다.

상단 발판
Create ONE transparent landscape raster sprite atlas for the original RelicEscape cave game. Reference 1 is our own B1 nest room; match its carved dark slate, crisp hand-inked edges, cool deep teal slate shadows and little patches of subdued yellow-green moss. Reference 2 is the designer's hand sketch; use only the FOUR horizontal shapes near the ceiling line: two long exterior platforms and two short inner platforms. Draw those four original usable ledge assets on a 2x2 contact sheet with a wide blank cross-shaped transparent gutter, absolutely no overlap. Top left is broad LEFT wall ledge with an irregular attached left stump and tapered right tip; top right is broad RIGHT wall ledge with a tapered left tip and an irregular attached right stump. Bottom left short inner stepping ledge and bottom right short inner stepping ledge have individually varied rounded layered slab silhouettes. All are shown in flat side-scroller elevation, clean mostly horizontal walking top with minimal tuft height, irregular rounded undersides of slate in two or three chunky layers. Broad platforms width to overall height ratio around 5:1, short ones around 3.2:1. Leave very generous empty space around each, and render all four entirely inside their quadrants. Restrained small moss cushions plus one or two thin hanging root tips where useful, avoid large vegetation, intense shine, straight sterile platform blocks, bright trim and background haze. True alpha transparency throughout background and gaps; no checkerboard, captions, guides, room, floor, falling rock, rails, characters, UI or extra assets.

중앙 낙하 암석
Create ONE isolated original 2D game prop on true transparent RGBA, portrait-oriented sprite composition. Reference 1 is our own RelicEscape B1 nest room as exact palette/material context. Reference 2 is the designer's hand sketch: interpret only the center hanging stone shape at top, a wide craggy crown that narrows toward a blunt asymmetrical heavy bottom, aligned to fall straight from the ceiling. Make a single massive detachable dark blue-gray slate boulder with a broad somewhat flat upper mating face, layered strong stone planes and weighty tapered lower half. One clearly readable branched fracture close to and across the upper third, mostly a dark seam with a very thin restrained warm gray exposed edge so the player sees where to strike; a few subsidiary shallow fractures only. Match the room's hand-inked visual language, crisp irregular deep dark outlines, cool teal bounce light on broad gray slate planes, limited discreet moss near the shoulders, subtle inky painted facets, no physically realistic phototexture. Avoid a neat triangular arrowhead, icicle, cute face, metal cage, mechanism, ropes, magical rune, gemstones, crystals, glow, dust or detached debris. All edges fully present with transparent padding, including above the broad joining surface. No ceiling or other scenery, platforms, boss, labels or fake checkerboard. Just the single boulder.
