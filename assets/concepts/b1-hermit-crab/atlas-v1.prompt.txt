Generation prompt
Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE high-resolution transparent RGBA sprite atlas for the original RelicEscape B1 hermit crab boss in a hand-painted 2D platform game. True transparent alpha, no backdrop, platform, ground shadow, frames, captions, guides, margins painted in, effects, separate particles or text. The six assets must be cleanly detached by transparent gutters, with one coherent design and camera. Landscape 3:2. Fixed straight-on orthographic fighting-game view, slightly shows the top of the curved shell, no perspective shift. Strong outer shapes for a cavern background. Materials: old sculpted slate/lilac conch with an off-center spiral, broad beveled growth ribs, small warm gray-brown worn edges, tiny restrained sage moss accents; a rust-colored crab with charcoal legs and weathered terracotta claws. Blue is reserved exclusively for the crystal props. Large readable planes, crisp painted silhouette, restrained detail, strong value separation, grounded proportions, original character artwork.
Top row occupies roughly 68% of the page height and contains exactly two big assets left and right, each centered within its half with at least five percent transparent gutter around it. Left: UPRIGHT BODY ONLY. A giant massive hermit crab's face on two short eye stalks, its four to six load-bearing jointed legs hoisting the same heavy conch well off the floor, brown-red head and body, and two clear rounded shoulder sockets facing camera near its lower outer sides. Two clearly visible shallow dark sockets/recesses near the left and right shoulders of the top rim, empty. These are the mount points for the separate blue crystals below; the upright shell's surfaces cover the equivalent positions so it reads as crystal-free. Same shape, width, color and exact material identity, slightly tilted downward on the floor, do not shrink it into a different shell.

At 68% across show a single detached vivid ice-blue faceted crystal cluster for one upper socket, and at 87% show a second matching detached ice-blue faceted crystal cluster at the same scale, subtly different lean. Each crystal is a crisp solid faceted sprite with one broad main blade and a short contiguous basal shard, no ground, particles, glow spill or shadows. Keep the per-asset edges intact and avoid any overlaps. The row of OFF-body claws and individual crystals is essential.
