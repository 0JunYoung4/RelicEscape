# B1 전당 전용 발판 프롬프트

내장 ImageGen의 투명 배경 모드로 네 형태를 각각 생성했다. 이후 각 원화를 첫 전투방의 플랫폼 원화와 SDL 화면을 스타일 참고로 넣어 다시 칠했다. 첫 전투방의 청록 그림자, 얇은 착지면 반사광, 잎 군집 표현을 적용했다. 프로젝트 참고 자료 SS-MG-03의 이끼 밀도 차이와 어두운 하부 실루엣, HK-GP07의 구조물과 식생 명암 분리를 참고했다. 최종 이미지는 투명 PNG로 저장했다.

## sluice-crown

### 형태 원화

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE original, production-ready isolated 2D side-view platform sprite for RelicEscape's flooded underground guardian hall. Transparent RGBA background, fully transparent outside silhouette and in holes. Landscape canvas. Center the single object horizontally with a generous transparent margin. Exact gameplay side view, an uninterrupted straight horizontal walkable top edge spans almost its full width and reads as a pale worn stone bevel; front face and irregular underside are visible below it. Match hand-painted, clean dark contours, large faceted stone planes and subtle texture of a dark fantasy 2D game: dark petroleum blue-gray slate, restrained cool gray-green top highlights, dark teal occlusion, only muted olive moss or aged brass if specified. No heavy outline, realistic room lighting, pedestal, extra objects, shadows outside object, top-down view, grid, text, game logos, glow, or visible backdrop. It must read clearly when reduced to 257–449 px wide. Entire shape cut out, edge-to-edge details kept inside the silhouette. Wide left outer platform, width about 6 times depth. A dislodged ancient sluice-gate lintel with a slight upward-curled carved ear at the far left (the flat floor stays below it), left-heavy stepped fluted stone hanging underneath, three broad worn channels engraved into the front, and a deep staggered break tapering into a single stone lip at its right end. Small patches of moss concentrated at the left crack only. Around 54% of the way from left to right, leave a solid, uninterrupted dark slate face directly below the walk line for an external rail bracket. Cut the lower silhouette into two large stepped masses with a visible concave gap off-center, no repeating brick pattern.
```
