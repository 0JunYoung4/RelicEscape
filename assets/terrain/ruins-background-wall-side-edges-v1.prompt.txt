Left edge
Use case: precise-object-edit. Generate a standalone LEFT SIDE edge tile for the transparent background stone wall of the 2D game RelicEscape. Image 1 is the source seamless wall tile and palette reference. Image 2 is the approved visual reference for the broken top-right corner; borrow only its restrained chipped stone treatment. Create one flat-on rectangular sprite in a 2:1 aspect ratio, framed from edge to edge. Preserve image 1's four horizontal courses, spacing, dark desaturated navy/slate stones, narrow mortar, understated blue-grey highlights and very sparse dark olive grime. The top, bottom, and RIGHT sides must stay straight and opaque all the way to the frame's edge where stone joins the neighboring wall, with course joints continuing naturally. Only the LEFT edge faces outward and terminates into true transparency. Give the exposed left edge a mostly vertical, subtly uneven outline across all four courses. The ends of individual bricks protrude and recess by different small amounts, with little discrete chips and notches at mortar joints. No large slant or staircase from top to bottom, no break along the horizontal edges, no rounded corners. Match the original hard pixel steps and unlit muted wall texture; don't add rim lighting, debris, trim, background, annotations or text. Show one single transparent PNG sprite.
