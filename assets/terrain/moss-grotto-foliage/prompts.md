# 이끼 동굴 식생 생성 프롬프트

내장 imagegen으로 한 종씩 생성했다. 원본 PNG의 투명도를 보존했다.

모든 프롬프트의 첫 번째 입력은 프로젝트의 기존 호박빛 열매 식생이다. 청록 그림자, 이끼빛 가장자리, 붓 질감을 맞추는 데 사용했다. 각 항목의 두 번째 입력은 잎 덩어리와 여백의 관계를 참고하는 데 사용했다.

Smooth hand painted gouache shadow shapes, subdued teal greens, deep charcoal-petrol shadows, restrained misty moss-olive tones, clean sculpted leaf contours. No painted background or matte, no glow, no luminous fruit, no spines, no serrated sawtooth tips, no soil, rock, platform, detached specks, outline stickers, text, grid, labels or extra variants in the image.
```text
Middle ground/floor decoration: use a muted olive-green upper edge sparingly on selected leaves, subdued enough to fit the current game backdrop. A ceiling-mounted asymmetrical drape with three irregularly spaced hanging tendrils sharing a short mossy crooked crown. A single longer soft S-curve hangs near the left of center, one sparse shorter stem to its right and a slight curled branch at one end. Small oval moss leaves gather in two loose clusters and gradually become sparse below. Overall a landscape 4:3 shape. The visually highest parts attach near the top of the canvas; leaves never touch the canvas edge. Keep the silhouette open, slim and easy to tuck against a rock ceiling.
```
